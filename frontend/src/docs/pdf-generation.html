Executive Performance Summary

Legacy Trust Bank has officially released its financial results for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026, delivering sustained growth across all key profitability and operational indicators.

The Bank recorded a total revenue of GH₵186.4 million during the quarter, reflecting a substantial expansion compared with GH₵159.8 million reported for the corresponding period in 2025.

Profit before tax stood firmly at GH₵42.6 million, translating to a resilient profit after tax of GH₵29.8 million for the reporting period.

Balance Sheet Strength

The Bank's total asset base expanded to GH₵4.85 billion as at 30 September 2026. High customer confidence supported deposit mobilization of GH₵3.72 billion, while prudent lending generated GH₵2.41 billion in active loans and advances.
