The Secure Touch

Imagine standing at a busy coffee shop counter on a rainy Monday morning and you pull out your phone to check your account balance before making a quick transfer. However, as you tap the screen, your mind goes blank.

Was your password the one with the capital 'L' and the special character, or the one you created last month after resetting it for the third time? After three incorrect attempts, your account is temporarily locked, the line behind you grows restless, and a simple errand turns into a frustrating delay.

It is a situation almost every mobile banking customer has experienced before. The digital world forced a compromise for a long time: either it's easy and less secure, or secure but more time-consuming.

Today, Legacy Trust Bank is officially getting rid of that compromise.

Everyone can attest to the fact that security was built on trust in physical keys, heavy iron vaults, and handshakes across a wooden desk.

As banking moved into the palms of our hands, physical keys became passwords, complex character requirements, and numbers.

The launch of Biometric Login on the Legacy Trust Mobile app marks a return to the olden days: having the key in your palms, literally, and not in your head where it can be forgotten.

Starting October 15, 2026, account holders can log into their mobile app using only a quick touch of a finger.

During the design process, we thought not just about how to make the process easy, but also about how to make it feel natural.

"Passcodes can be forgotten, but can you forget your fingerprint?"

Biometric authentication turns your unique physical markers into the most sophisticated key.

Effortless Access

With the debate on how it differs from regular logins, for everyday app users, the uniqueness is effortless access.

The moments spent typing passwords or waiting for authentication codes and one-time passcodes are replaced by an instant, smooth unlock.

The process of checking balances, verifying recent purchases, or approving urgent wire transfers now takes less than a second. What was once hectic is now completely seamless.

Uncompromising Protection

Behind this seamless process sits an uncompromising layer of protection. Biometric verification dramatically reduces the risk of phishing and unauthorized account access, providing peace of mind every time you open the app.

Your Data Stays Yours

Your biological data stays exclusively yours. Legacy Trust Bank never stores fingerprints on central servers.

Verification happens exclusively on your device's built-in secure hardware enclave, ensuring complete privacy, zero transmission, and total security.

Fast Activation

Activating biometric authentication requires no complex setup and takes less than a minute:

  1. 1 Update: Download the latest version of Legacy Trust Mobile from the App Store or Google Play Store.
  2. 2 Log In: Open the app and log in using your current username and password.
  3. 3 Enable: Navigate to Settings > Security Options, toggle on Enable Biometric Login, and follow the brief prompt to complete authorization.

For customers who prefer traditional login methods, password and PIN access will remain fully available alongside the new biometric feature.

As digital threats evolve, Legacy Trust Bank remains committed to building tools that keep your wealth secure without getting in the way of your life.
